Our operation: a groin hernia repair. Germany does about a quarter of a million a year.

In England and Sweden, about four in five patients go home the same day. In Germany, closer to one in seven — partly because the old payment rules made the overnight stay the default. For most patients that night isn't needed; studies of carefully selected patients find no difference in complications.

So from twenty twenty-four Germany set one price for this operation and many others — the same whether the patient goes home or stays the night. For a simple repair this year: about two thousand euros.

A sensible fix. So why is everyone at the table unhappy?
