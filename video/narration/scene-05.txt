When a patient goes home, a bed empties. On paper, a few hundred euros vanish from the bill. But the nurse is still on the rota. Little money is saved — until the hospital truly changes what it spends.

And there is the part that cannot sleep. For years, routine operations helped to pay for it.

Two German hospitals in three are losing money. A payment just for staying ready is now law — but it arrives in full only in twenty thirty.

The new price is already here.
