A groin hernia is a weak point in the wall of the body. Most patients need no night in hospital to repair it.

In Scandinavia and Britain, most go home the same day. In Germany, most have stayed the night.

Now the rules have changed. For a simple case, the hospital is paid one fixed price — whether the patient goes home that evening, or stays until morning.

Who is fit to go home remains a doctor's decision. The price simply stops paying for nights that are not needed.
